Solution overview: a governed AI lending assistant

An agentic assistant that answers small-business lending-policy questions and prequalifies applicants in seconds. The model writes; deterministic code decides; humans sign off on high-value loans; and every answer is cited, explainable and logged. The same design runs on AWS, Azure or GCP.

The problem

• Prequalification takes days of manual back-and-forth
• Policy is applied inconsistently across bankers
• Fair-lending exposure: decisions must be explainable and free of protected-attribute bias (ECOA / Reg B)
• AI pilots stall because risk teams can't see how the model decides or prove it is governed

The solution

• A LangGraph agent with guardrails, retrieval, tools, a self-critique loop and human review
• Lending math runs in code; the LLM only explains results with policy citations
• A model gateway routes each request to the cheapest model that meets quality and data rules
• Fairness, drift and eval gates are wired into CI/CD, so a non-compliant model can't ship

Design targets

• Prequalification answer in seconds, not days
• 100% of answers cite the governing policy
• Adverse-action reasons generated for every decline or conditional decision
• 60–70% lower model spend than all-premium routing (simulated in tab 05)
• Bias caught at build time, not in an audit

Delivery roadmap

Phase 1 · Pilot (6–8 wks)
Internal policy Q&A for bankers. RAG over lending policy, guardrails, tracing and the eval suite in CI. Success measure: answer accuracy and citation rate.
Phase 2 · Prequalification
Customer-facing prequalification with deterministic tools, critic loop and human review over $250k. Fairness gate and evidence pack sign-off with compliance.
Phase 3 · Scale
Multi-provider routing for cost, drift-triggered retraining, governance-platform integration, and reuse of the agent pattern for servicing and collections.

Responsible AI: fairness testing, explainability, governance evidence

  
A synthetic portfolio of 1,000 applicants scored by a transparent logistic model. The protected attribute is never a model input, but toggle the zip-region feature and watch a proxy reintroduce disparate impact. Explanations are exact per-feature contributions (for a linear model these equal SHAP values) and drive adverse-action reasons. The evidence pack exports as JSON for a governance platform.
